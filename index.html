📐 فرمول‌های محاسباتی
ATR Stop = ATR(14) × 1.5
Risk Amount = Capital × Risk% / 100
Position Size = Risk Amount / ATR Stop
Fee = Position × Entry × 0.001 (0.05% × 2 sides)
R:R = (TP1 - Entry) / (Entry - SL)
Expectancy = (Win% × Avg Win) - (Loss% × Avg Loss)
